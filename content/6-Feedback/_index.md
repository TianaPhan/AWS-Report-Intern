### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The Working environment is quite comfortable and friendly. People are easy to communicate with and willing to help when I encounter difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is also tidy and conducive to concentration.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors were very dedicated, explaining things clearly when I didn't understand and always encouraging me to ask further questions, which helped me gain a deeper understanding. The admin team also provided quick support with documentation and related issues.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The AWS internship and project provided invaluable practical experience. I gained a better understanding of how to deploy web applications on the cloud through services such as EC2, VPC, RDS, S3, and IAM.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the process, I encountered quite a few problems that required me to figure out how to solve them myself. This helped me improve my debugging skills and technical thinking. At the same time, I realized that I need to learn a lot more if I want to pursue this field long-term.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
Everyone works in a relaxed yet serious manner when needed. When there's an urgent task, everyone supports each other to get it done. This makes me feel comfortable and not overly stressed.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides internship allowances and flexible working hours when needed. Additionally, participation in events helps gain valuable practical knowledge.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- For the University: I suggest that the Faculty incorporate more practical cloud computing courses and hands-on labs related to AWS services into the curriculum. Organizing more industry-sponsored workshops or seminars would also help students bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world industrial demands.

- For the Company / Internship Program: The internship program was highly valuable. I recommend that the company continue to expand the scope of mentorship to include more deep-dive sessions on "Infrastructure as Code" (IaC) tools like Terraform or CloudFormation. Furthermore, providing more opportunities for interns to participate in the entire lifecycle of a production project would significantly enhance our practical problem-solving skills.
